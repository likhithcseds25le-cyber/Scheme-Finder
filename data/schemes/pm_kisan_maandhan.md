# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)

**Ministry / Department:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India (in partnership with Life Insurance Corporation of India - LIC)  
**Scheme Category:** Social Security, Pension, Agriculture, Old-Age Protection  
**Official Website:** https://maandhan.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-PENS-006  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY) is an old-age pension scheme for Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs). It is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme where the Government of India provides an equal matching contribution to the monthly subscription paid by the farmer.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Pension Details
- **Assured Monthly Pension:** Minimum **Rs. 3,000 per month** to the beneficiary upon attaining the age of **60 years**.
- **Family Pension:** If the subscriber dies while receiving pension, the spouse is entitled to receive **50% of the pension** (i.e., Rs. 1,500/month) as family pension (applicable only to spouse, not children).
- **Matching Government Contribution:** The Central Government contributes an equal amount (50:50 ratio) directly into the pension fund managed by LIC.
- **Monthly Subscription:** Based on entry age (between 18 and 40 years), the monthly contribution ranges from **Rs. 55 to Rs. 200 per month**.
  - Age 18: Rs. 55/month
  - Age 30: Rs. 105/month
  - Age 40: Rs. 200/month

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Beneficiary:** Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs) who own cultivable agricultural land up to **2 hectares (approx. 4.94 acres)** as per state land records.
2. **Entry Age:** Between **18 years and 40 years** (inclusive).
3. **Residency:** Citizen and resident of India.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria
The following categories cannot join PM-KMY:
1. Farmers covered under other social security schemes such as National Pension Scheme (NPS), Employees' State Insurance (ESI), Employees' Provident Fund (EPFO), or Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM).
2. Farmers who have opted for Pradhan Mantri Laghu Vyapari Maandhan.
3. Institutional landholders.
4. Individuals who paid Income Tax in any past assessment year.
5. Serving or retired central/state government employees or PSU officials.

## 5. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Mandatory for digital identity and biometric verification.
2. **Savings Bank Account Passbook / PM-KISAN Account Details:** Bank account IFSC and account number with auto-debit consent.
3. **Land Ownership Proof:** Copy of Khasra / Khatoni / 7/12 extract proving agricultural land ownership up to 2 hectares.
4. **Mobile Number:** For SMS alerts and authentication.

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Procedure

### Via Common Service Centre (CSC)
1. **Visit Nearest CSC:** The farmer visits the nearest Common Service Centre with Aadhaar, Bank passbook, and land ownership document.
2. **CSC Portal Login:** The CSC Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) logs into https://maandhan.in.
3. **Biometric Authentication:** Enter Aadhaar number, farmer's name, date of birth, and complete biometric fingerprint verification.
4. **Bank Auto-Debit Setup:** Enter bank account details and submit auto-debit mandate form so monthly contribution is deducted automatically (can also be deducted directly from PM-KISAN installment if opted).
5. **Issue of Kisan Pension Card:** System calculates monthly contribution based on entry age. Initial contribution is paid in cash to CSC.
6. **Print Card:** A unique Kisan Pension Account Number (KPAN) and digital **Kisan Card** is printed and handed over to the farmer immediately.
