# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

**Ministry / Department:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India  
**Scheme Category:** Rural Housing, Financial Subsidy, Basic Infrastructure  
**Official Website:** https://pmayg.nic.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-HSG-003  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G) aims to provide "Housing for All" in rural areas by constructing pucca houses with basic amenities like piped drinking water, electricity connection, and LPG connection (via PM Ujjwala Yojana) to all houseless families and families living in kutcha or dilapidated houses.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Financial Assistance
- **Unit Assistance for Plain Areas:** Rs. 1,20,000 per house.
- **Unit Assistance for Hilly/Difficult/IAP Areas:** Rs. 1,30,000 per house (including North Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu & Kashmir, and Ladakh).
- **Additional MGNREGA Wage Support:** Beneficiaries receive 90 to 95 days of unskilled labour wages under MGNREGA (approx. Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 25,000).
- **Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) Assistance:** Up to Rs. 12,000 dedicated assistance for construction of household toilet (IHHL).
- **Minimum House Size:** 25 square meters (including dedicated hygienic cooking space).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Group:** Homeless families or families living in zero, one, or two-room houses with kutcha wall and kutcha roof as per SECC 2011 and Awaas+ survey list.
2. **Priority Criteria:**
   - Houseless households.
   - Families with no adult male member aged 16 to 59.
   - Female-headed households without adult working males.
   - Families with disabled members and no able-bodied adult member.
   - SC, ST, and Minorities in extreme poverty.
   - Landless households dependent on daily manual labour.
3. **Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of a rural area in the Gram Panchayat.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria
Households possessing any of the following 13 assets/conditions are excluded:
1. Motorized two/three/four wheelers, fishing boats.
2. Mechanized three/four wheeler agricultural equipment (tractor, harvester).
3. Kisan Credit Card with credit limit >= Rs. 50,000.
4. Any family member who is a regular government employee.
5. Households paying income tax or professional tax.
6. Households with refrigerators or landline phones.
7. Owning 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land with at least 1 irrigation equipment.
8. Owning 5 acres or more of irrigated land for 2 or more crop seasons.
9. Owning 7.5 acres or more of land with at least one irrigation equipment.

## 5. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Proof of identity of all adult family members.
2. **Bank Account Details:** Aadhaar-linked active bank account or post office savings account.
3. **MGNREGA Job Card Number:** For wage entitlement linkage.
4. **Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM-G) Registration Number:** For toilet assistance.
5. **Land Ownership / House Site Document:** Record showing ownership of the homestead plot where house will be constructed.
6. **Affidavit / Consent Form:** Declaration stating the applicant does not own any other pucca house in India.

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Procedure
1. **Identification via Gram Sabha / Awaas+:**
   - Beneficiaries are identified from the SECC-2011 list and verified by the local **Gram Sabha**.
   - Gram Sabha approves the prioritized beneficiary list.
2. **Registration on AwaasSoft:**
   - Village Panchayat Secretary / Block Development Officer (BDO) registers the approved beneficiary on the **AwaasSoft** portal (https://pmayg.nic.in).
   - Enter bank account, Aadhaar number, and mobile number.
3. **Geo-tagging of Existing Site:**
   - Gram Rozgar Sahayak (GRS) visits the site and uploads geotagged photograph of the existing kutcha house using the AwaasApp mobile app.
4. **Sanction and Fund Release:**
   - House sanction order issued by Block Development Officer.
   - Financial assistance released in 3 or 4 stages directly to beneficiary's bank account via DBT:
     - Installment 1: At registration/sanction (commencement).
     - Installment 2: Upon reaching plinth level.
     - Installment 3: Upon reaching lintel/roof level.
     - Installment 4: On completion of house and toilet.
