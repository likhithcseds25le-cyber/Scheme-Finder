# Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)

**Ministry / Department:** State Health Assurance Society (SHAS), Public Health Department, Government of Maharashtra  
**Scheme Category:** State Health Insurance, Cashless Medical Care, Secondary & Tertiary Treatment  
**Official Website:** https://www.jeevandayee.gov.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-MH-HLTH-009  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY) is a flagship health insurance scheme of the Government of Maharashtra providing end-to-end cashless medical treatment to citizens of Maharashtra across 1,356 identified surgical and medical procedures through an empanelled network of government and private hospitals.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Coverage
- **Coverage Amount:** Cashless treatment of up to **Rs. 5,00,000 per family per year** on a family floater basis (expanded uniformly across Maharashtra).
- **Renal Transplant Exception:** Up to **Rs. 6,00,000** for kidney transplantation procedures.
- **Coverage Scope:** Includes hospital bed charges, nursing, consultation fees, surgical procedures, ICU, diagnostic investigations, implants, medicines, and post-discharge consultations.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Domicile / Residency:** Must be a permanent resident of Maharashtra holding a valid Domicile Certificate or valid Maharashtra-issued Ration Card.
2. **Target Groups:**
   - **Category A:** Families holding Yellow Ration Card or Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) Ration Card or Annapurna Card.
   - **Category B:** Families holding Orange Ration Card (annual family income up to Rs. 1 Lakh).
   - **Category C:** Farmers from 14 distressed agricultural districts of Maharashtra (Amravati, Akola, Aurangabad, Beed, Buldhana, Hingoli, Jalna, Nanded, Latur, Osmanabad, Parbhani, Wardha, Washim, and Yavatmal) holding White Ration Card / 7/12 extract.
   - **Category D:** Senior citizens and children in government-run orphanages/ashrams.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria
1. Non-residents of Maharashtra.
2. Individuals already possessing private health insurance with sum insured greater than Rs. 5 Lakhs (cannot double-claim).
3. Persons paying income tax above the specified exemption slabs unless eligible under distressed farmer category.

## 5. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Ration Card:** Valid Yellow, Orange, AAY, or White (for distressed farmer districts) Ration Card.
2. **Photo ID Proof:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Pan Card.
3. **Domicile Certificate / Tahsildar Income Certificate:** If required for income slab validation.
4. **7/12 Land Extract:** For farmers in distressed agricultural districts.

## 6. Step-by-Step Hospital Admission & Treatment Procedure
1. **Hospital Visit:**
   - Patient visits any empanelled Network Hospital (Government or Private) in Maharashtra.
2. **Arogyamitra Assistance:**
   - Approach the **Arogyamitra Help Desk** at the entrance of the hospital.
   - Present Ration Card and Aadhaar Card.
3. **Registration & Diagnosis:**
   - Arogyamitra registers the patient on the SHAS portal and facilitates medical examination by doctors.
4. **Pre-Authorization Request:**
   - Hospital sends online pre-authorization request with diagnostic reports to SHAS Medical Division.
   - Medical Specialist verifies and approves pre-authorization within 12 hours (immediate emergency pre-authorization available).
5. **Cashless Treatment & Discharge:**
   - Hospital provides completely cashless treatment, surgery, and medications.
   - Patient discharged with 10 days of free follow-up medicines without paying any cash.
