# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

**Ministry / Department:** National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India  
**Scheme Category:** Healthcare, Health Insurance, Social Protection  
**Official Website:** https://pmjay.gov.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-HLTH-002  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest health assurance scheme fully financed by the government. It aims to provide free and cashless secondary and tertiary healthcare coverage to over 12 crore vulnerable rural and urban families (approx. 55 crore beneficiaries), covering bottom 40% of the Indian population as identified in Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011). Recently expanded to cover all senior citizens aged 70 and above regardless of income.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Coverage
- **Coverage Amount:** Cashless health cover of up to **Rs. 5,00,000 per family per year** for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
- **Portability:** Nationwide cashless and paperless access at any empanelled public or private hospital across India.
- **Pre & Post Hospitalization:** Covers medical examination, treatment, consultation, pre-hospitalization (up to 3 days), medicine, diagnostic laboratory services, hospital accommodation, food services, complication during treatment, and post-hospitalization follow-up care (up to 15 days).
- **No Family Size Cap:** No restriction on family size, age, or gender. Pre-existing medical conditions covered from Day 1.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Rural Beneficiaries (SECC Criteria):**
   - Families living in only one room with kucha walls and roof (D1).
   - Families with no adult member between age 16 to 59 (D2).
   - Female-headed households with no adult male member between 16 to 59 (D3).
   - Families having member with disability and no able-bodied adult member (D4).
   - SC/ST households (D5).
   - Landless households deriving major part of their income from manual casual labour (D7).
2. **Urban Beneficiaries (Occupational Categories):**
   - Ragpickers, Beggars, Domestic workers.
   - Street vendors, Cobblers, Hawkers, Other service providers on streets.
   - Construction workers, Plumbers, Masons, Painters, Security guards, Welders.
   - Sweepers, Sanitation workers, Mali.
   - Home-based workers, Artisans, Handicrafts workers, Tailors.
   - Transport workers, Drivers, Conductors, Helpers to drivers and conductors, Cart pullers, Rickshaw pullers.
   - Shop workers, Assistants, Peons in small establishments, Attendants, Waiters.
   - Mechanics, Electricians, Repair workers, Assemblers.
   - Washermen, Chowkidars.
3. **Senior Citizens (70+ Extension):**
   - All senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for up to Rs. 5 lakh dedicated top-up health cover regardless of socio-economic status.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria
1. Owning motorized 2/3/4 wheeler or fishing boat.
2. Owning mechanized 3/4 wheeler agricultural equipment.
3. Having Kisan Credit Card with credit limit of Rs. 50,000 or above.
4. Household member who is a government employee.
5. Households with non-agricultural enterprises registered with government.
6. Any member of the family paying income tax or professional tax.
7. Owning a pucca house with more than 3 rooms.

## 5. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Or official photo ID proof for identity verification.
2. **Ration Card:** NFSA Ration Card or SECC registration proof to establish family relationship.
3. **Mobile Number:** For OTP verification.
4. **Certificate proof:** SC/ST certificate or Disability Certificate (if applying under specific category).

## 6. Step-by-Step Application & Ayushman Card Generation Procedure
1. **Check Eligibility Online:**
   - Visit https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in (Beneficiary Portal) or PM-JAY App.
   - Select login as 'Beneficiary', enter Mobile number, authenticate via OTP.
   - Search by State, Scheme (PMJAY), and search parameter (Aadhaar Number, Family ID, Ration Card, or Location).
2. **Identity Verification & e-KYC:**
   - If family is listed, click 'e-KYC' next to the member's name.
   - Complete Aadhaar OTP or Biometric/Facial authentication.
   - Capture live applicant photograph.
3. **Card Approval & Download:**
   - Upon successful verification, the Ayushman Card (Golden Card / PMJAY Card) is generated.
   - Download PVC digital card instantly.
4. **Hospital Assistance (Offline):**
   - Walk into any empanelled hospital and approach the **Ayushman Mitra** helpdesk at the hospital reception.
   - Present Aadhaar and Ration Card to verify eligibility and initiate cashless admission.
