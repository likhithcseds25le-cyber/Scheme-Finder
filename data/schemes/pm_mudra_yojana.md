# Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

**Ministry / Department:** Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India  
**Scheme Category:** Financial Inclusion, Business Loans, Micro Enterprises  
**Official Website:** https://www.mudra.org.in / https://www.udyamimitra.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-FIN-004  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA (Micro Units Development & Refinance Agency) Yojana is designed to provide formal institutional credit up to Rs. 20 Lakhs to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises. The scheme aims to fund the unfunded small businesses, promote self-employment, and generate job opportunities.

## 2. Loan Categories and Financial Benefits
Loans are categorized based on business growth stage and funding requirements:
- **Shishu Category:** Loans up to **Rs. 50,000** (For startups, new entrepreneurs, and initial setup).
- **Kishore Category:** Loans above **Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 5,00,000** (For purchasing machinery, raw material, and working capital for existing micro businesses).
- **Tarun Category:** Loans above **Rs. 5,00,000 up to Rs. 10,00,000** (Expanded to **Rs. 20 Lakhs** for established entrepreneurs with good track record under Union Budget 2024).
- **Collateral-Free:** No collateral or third-party guarantee required. Covered under Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU).
- **Interest Rates:** As per RBI guidelines and individual bank lending rates (typically between 8.5% and 12%).
- **Repayment Period:** Flexible repayment tenor up to 5 to 7 years.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Beneficiary:** Any Indian citizen who has a business plan for a non-farm income-generating activity such as manufacturing, processing, trading, or service sector.
2. **Eligible Activities:**
   - Small manufacturing units, artisans, food processing, packaging.
   - Retail shops, grocery, tailoring, garment work, hair salons, repair shops.
   - Transport vehicles (autos, e-rickshaws, small commercial transport).
   - Allied agricultural activities: Dairy, poultry, bee-keeping, fisheries, agri-clinics.
3. **Credit History:** Must not be a defaulter with any bank or financial institution. Clean CIBIL / credit bureau record.
4. **Age:** Minimum 18 years, maximum 65 years.

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport.
2. **Residence Proof:** Recent utility bill (electricity/telephone), Ration card, or Aadhaar.
3. **Business Address Proof:** Shop and Establishment Certificate, GST registration, or Trade license (if existing).
4. **Passport Size Photographs:** 2 recent photos of applicant / partners / directors.
5. **Bank Account Statements:** Past 6 months statement from existing bank account.
6. **Business Plan / Project Report:** Details of machinery, equipment quotation, cost estimates, and expected turnover (Mandatory for Kishore and Tarun loans).
7. **Proof of Category:** SC/ST/OBC certificate if applicable for priority sector statistics.

## 5. Step-by-Step Application Procedure

### Online Method (Via Udyamimitra Portal)
1. **Visit Portal:** Go to https://www.udyamimitra.in and click on **'Apply for Mudra Loan'**.
2. **User Registration:** Register with Name, Email, and Mobile number with OTP authentication.
3. **Select Loan Category:** Choose *Shishu* (<= 50k), *Kishore* (50k - 5L), or *Tarun* (5L - 20L).
4. **Fill Application Form:** Fill in personal details, business enterprise information, existing loans, and required loan amount.
5. **Upload Documents:** Upload KYC documents, quotation of machinery/goods, and bank statement.
6. **Select Lending Institutions:** Choose preferred Public Sector Bank, Private Bank, Regional Rural Bank (RRB), or NBFC.
7. **Track Application:** Application number is generated and routed to the chosen branch for field verification and credit sanction.

### Offline Method (Branch Visit)
1. Visit any nearby commercial bank, Regional Rural Bank, Small Finance Bank, or MFI branch.
2. Request the official PMMY Loan Application Form (Shishu or Kishore/Tarun).
3. Attach required KYC, quotation of equipment/goods to be purchased, and submit to the Branch Manager.
4. Following appraisal and sanction, the Mudra Card (RuPay Debit Card) with overdraft/credit limit is issued.
