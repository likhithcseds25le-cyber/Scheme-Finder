# Stand-Up India Scheme

**Ministry / Department:** Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India (operated through SIDBI)  
**Scheme Category:** Entrepreneurship, Bank Loans, Women Empowerment, SC/ST Welfare  
**Official Website:** https://www.standupmitra.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-ENTR-007  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Stand-Up India Scheme facilitates bank loans between **Rs. 10 Lakhs and Rs. 1 Crore** to at least one Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) borrower and at least one Woman borrower per bank branch of all scheduled commercial banks for setting up a greenfield (new) enterprise.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Financial Structure
- **Loan Amount:** Composite loan (including term loan and working capital) between **Rs. 10 Lakhs and Rs. 100 Lakhs (1 Crore)**.
- **Coverage of Project Cost:** Up to **85% of total project cost** (Borrower margin requirement reduced to minimum 15% with convergence of other central/state subsidies).
- **Repayment Period:** Repayable in **7 years** with a maximum moratorium period of up to **18 months**.
- **Interest Rate:** Lowest applicable rate of the bank for that category (Base Rate / MCLR + 3% + tenor premium).
- **Security:** Collateral or guarantee of Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Stand-Up India Loans (CGFSIL).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Group:** SC / ST and / or Woman entrepreneurs.
2. **Age:** Minimum 18 years of age.
3. **Enterprise Nature:** Must be a **Greenfield enterprise** (i.e., the first-time venture of the beneficiary in manufacturing, services, agri-allied activities, or trading sector).
4. **Shareholding Rule (For Non-Individual Enterprises):** In case of non-individual enterprises (such as private limited company, partnership firm, or LLP), at least **51% of the shareholding and controlling stake** must be held by either an SC/ST or a Woman entrepreneur.
5. **Credit History:** The borrower should not be in default to any bank or financial institution.

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Identity & Address Proof:** Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, PAN Card.
2. **Caste Certificate:** SC or ST certificate issued by competent revenue authority (not applicable for general women applicants).
3. **Project Report:** Detailed project report (DPR) containing cost of project, projected cash flow, technical feasibility, and financial viability.
4. **Proof of Business Premises:** Lease agreement, rent deed, or ownership deed for proposed factory/office/shop.
5. **Promoter Contribution Proof:** Proof of 15% margin money / own contribution.
6. **Partnership Deed / MoA & AoA:** If applying as partnership firm, LLP, or company.

## 5. Step-by-Step Application Procedure

### Online Method (Via Stand-Up Mitra Portal)
1. **Access Portal:** Visit https://www.standupmitra.in.
2. **Register as Trainee or Ready Borrower:**
   - *Trainee Borrower:* If handholding support is needed (skilling, DPR preparation, financial literacy).
   - *Ready Borrower:* If business plan and margin money are already prepared.
3. **Fill Application Form:** Fill in personal details, caste category, proposed enterprise activity, project cost, and preferred bank branch.
4. **Upload Documents:** Upload DPR, PAN, Aadhaar, Caste certificate, and promoter margin proof.
5. **Connect with Handholding Agencies:** Connect with SIDBI, NABARD, DIC (District Industries Centre), or MSME-DI if guidance is required.
6. **Bank Branch Processing:** Application is forwarded to the selected scheduled commercial bank branch for credit assessment, site inspection, and loan sanction.
