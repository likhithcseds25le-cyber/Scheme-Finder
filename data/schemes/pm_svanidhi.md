# PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM-SVANidhi)

**Ministry / Department:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), Government of India  
**Scheme Category:** Urban Livelihood, Micro-credit, Street Vendors  
**Official Website:** https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-URBN-005  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
PM-SVANidhi is a Central Sector micro-credit scheme launched to facilitate collateral-free working capital loans to urban street vendors, hawkers, thelewala, and roadside artisans. The scheme aims to help vendors restart their livelihoods, incentivize timely repayment with interest subsidies, and reward digital transactions with cashback.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Financial Details
- **First Tranche Loan:** Collateral-free working capital loan of **up to Rs. 10,000** with 1-year repayment tenure.
- **Second Tranche Loan:** On timely or early repayment of the 1st loan, eligible for **up to Rs. 20,000** loan with 18-month tenure.
- **Third Tranche Loan:** On timely repayment of the 2nd loan, eligible for **up to Rs. 50,000** loan with 36-month tenure.
- **Interest Subsidy:** **7% interest subsidy per annum** credited quarterly directly to the vendor's bank account via DBT upon regular repayments.
- **Cashback for Digital Transactions:** Monthly cashback of **up to Rs. 100** (Rs. 1,200 annually) for conducting digital payment transactions through UPI/QR codes.
- **No Penalty:** No prepayment penalty on early repayment.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Beneficiary:** Street vendors, hawkers, fruit/vegetable sellers, fast food stalls, chai/snack stalls, laundry workers, barbers, cobblers vending in urban areas or peri-urban/rural areas vending in neighboring cities.
2. **Identification Criteria (Any ONE of the following):**
   - Possessing Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Identity Card issued by Urban Local Body (ULB) / Town Vending Committee (TVC).
   - Identified in the municipal survey but not yet issued Identity Card.
   - Street vendors left out of the ULB survey or who started vending after completion of survey, possessing a **Letter of Recommendation (LoR)** issued by the ULB / Town Vending Committee.
3. **Residency & Age:** Indian resident operating in urban jurisdiction; age 18 years and above.

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Or voter ID / driving license for identity.
2. **Vending Proof:** Certificate of Vending (CoV), Vendor ID Card, or Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by the ULB.
3. **Bank Account Details:** Passbook copy or cancelled cheque linked to Aadhaar.
4. **Mobile Number:** Linked with Aadhaar for OTP verification.

## 5. Step-by-Step Application Procedure

### Online Method (Via PM-SVANidhi Portal or Mobile App)
1. **Visit Portal:** Go to https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in.
2. **Click 'Apply for Loan':** Select tranche (10K / 20K / 50K).
3. **Mobile & OTP Authentication:** Enter Aadhaar-linked mobile number and verify via OTP.
4. **Select Vendor Status:** Choose category (Surveyed vendor with ID, or Request Letter of Recommendation).
5. **Enter KYC Details:** Provide Aadhaar number, complete biometric or OTP KYC.
6. **Select Preferred Lending Institution:** Choose Bank/NBFC/MFI where you maintain an account or prefer loan disbursement.
7. **Submit Application:** Download confirmation receipt. The bank branch processes the application and disburses funds within 7-14 working days.

### Offline Method (Through Urban Local Body or CSC)
1. Visit the local Municipal Corporation / Municipality office or Common Service Centre (CSC).
2. Contact the Day-NULM / City Mission Management Unit (CMMU) desk.
3. Operators assist in generating Letter of Recommendation (LoR) and submitting online loan application form.
