# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

**Ministry / Department:** Ministry of Finance (under Beti Bachao Beti Padhao initiative), Government of India  
**Scheme Category:** Girl Child Welfare, High-Interest Savings Scheme, Tax Exemption  
**Official Website:** https://www.indiapost.gov.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-CHLD-008  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a government-backed small savings scheme designed specifically to support parents in building a dedicated fund for the education and marriage expenses of their female child. It offers one of the highest government guaranteed interest rates with Triple Tax Exemption (EEE).

## 2. Benefit Amount and Financial Features
- **Interest Rate:** 8.2% per annum (compounded annually, as declared quarterly by the Ministry of Finance).
- **Deposit Limits:**
  - Minimum deposit: **Rs. 250 per financial year**.
  - Maximum deposit: **Rs. 1,50,000 per financial year**.
- **Deposit Period:** Deposits can be made for **15 years** from the date of account opening.
- **Maturity Period:** Account matures upon completion of **21 years** from the date of opening, or upon marriage of the girl child after attaining age 18.
- **Tax Benefit:** **EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt)**:
  - Principal deposit eligible for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act up to Rs. 1.5 Lakh.
  - Annual interest accrued is completely tax-free.
  - Full maturity proceeds are 100% tax-free.
- **Partial Withdrawal:** Up to 50% of the account balance as of previous financial year can be withdrawn for higher education expenses after the girl child turns 18 or passes Class 10.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Beneficiary:** Girl child who is an Indian resident.
2. **Account Opening Age:** From birth up to the age of **10 years**.
3. **Account Operator:** Opened and operated by natural or legal guardian until girl child reaches 18 years, after which the girl child operates it.
4. **Number of Accounts:** Only **one account per girl child**, and maximum of **two girl children per family** (exception allowed for triplets or second birth of twins).

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Birth Certificate of Girl Child:** Official birth certificate issued by municipal authority or registrar of births.
2. **Guardian's Identity Proof:** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport.
3. **Guardian's Address Proof:** Aadhaar, Utility Bill, or Ration Card.
4. **Photographs:** Passport size photos of the guardian and girl child.
5. **Medical Certificate:** In case of second birth of twins/triplets to establish exception.

## 5. Step-by-Step Application Procedure
1. **Visit Post Office or Authorized Commercial Bank:**
   - Account can be opened at any India Post Office branch or authorized public/private commercial bank (e.g., SBI, PNB, BoB, HDFC, ICICI, Axis).
2. **Obtain SSY Account Opening Form (Form 1):**
   - Collect Form 1 from the branch counter or download from the India Post official portal.
3. **Fill Form & Attach Documents:**
   - Fill in details of girl child (name, date of birth) and guardian (name, Aadhaar, address).
   - Attach self-attested photocopies of Birth Certificate, Guardian's Aadhaar & PAN.
4. **Make Initial Deposit:**
   - Pay the opening deposit amount (minimum Rs. 250) in cash, cheque, or demand draft.
5. **Receive SSY Passbook:**
   - The post office/bank issues a dedicated Sukanya Samriddhi Passbook with account number, holder details, and transaction records.
   - Future annual deposits can be made in cash, cheque, or via online NetBanking/IPPB app.
