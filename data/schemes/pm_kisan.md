# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

**Ministry / Department:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India  
**Scheme Category:** Agriculture, Financial Support, Direct Benefit Transfer  
**Official Website:** https://pmkisan.gov.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-AGRI-001  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme launched to supplement the financial needs of land-holding Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs) across India. It provides income support to eligible farmer families to help procure inputs for agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Payment Schedule
- **Financial Benefit:** Rs. 6,000 per year per eligible farmer family.
- **Disbursement Mode:** Transferred directly into bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 every four months:
  - Period 1: April to July
  - Period 2: August to November
  - Period 3: December to March

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
A citizen must satisfy ALL the following criteria:
1. **Occupation:** Farmer / Landholder actively engaged in agricultural activities.
2. **Land Holding:** Small and Marginal Farmer families who own cultivable landholding up to 2 hectares (approximately 4.94 acres) in their name as per state land revenue records.
3. **Citizenship & Residency:** Indian citizen residing in any State or Union Territory.
4. **Age Criteria:** Minimum age 18 years. (Family definition: Husband, wife, and minor children).
5. **Mandatory Compliance:** Aadhaar-linked active bank account and completion of e-KYC on the PM-KISAN portal.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria (Who is NOT Eligible)
The following categories are strictly excluded from PM-KISAN benefits:
1. Institutional landholders.
2. Farmer families holding constitutional posts (former or present).
3. Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors of Municipal Corporations, and Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
4. Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries, Departments, and PSUs (excluding Multi-Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees).
5. All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000 or more (excluding Group D / Class IV).
6. Any person who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
7. Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies and carrying on practice.

## 5. Mandatory Documents Required
To apply for PM-KISAN, the applicant must produce:
1. **Aadhaar Card:** Mandatory for identification and e-KYC authentication.
2. **Proof of Citizenship / Domicile:** State residency proof.
3. **Land Ownership Documents:** Land registration papers, Khatauni / Khasra / 7/12 land record extract showing applicant's name.
4. **Bank Account Passbook:** Bank account linked with Aadhaar and NPCI mapped for Direct Benefit Transfer.
5. **Mobile Number:** Active mobile number linked with Aadhaar for OTP verification.
6. **Self-declaration:** Undertaking confirming non-exclusion under Income Tax or government employment rules.

## 6. Step-by-Step Application Procedure

### Online Method (Via PM-KISAN Official Portal)
1. **Visit Official Portal:** Go to https://pmkisan.gov.in and click on **'Farmer Corner'**.
2. **New Farmer Registration:** Click on **'New Farmer Registration'** and choose *Rural Farmer Registration* or *Urban Farmer Registration*.
3. **Aadhaar Authentication:** Enter Aadhaar Number, select State, enter captcha code, and click 'Send OTP'.
4. **Fill Profile Details:** Fill in personal details (Name, Gender, Category: General/SC/ST/OBC), IFSC code, bank account number, and district/sub-district/village.
5. **Upload Land Details:** Enter Survey/Khasra number, Khata number, land area in hectares, and upload digital copy of land record.
6. **Submit & Note Reference:** Submit the application form and note down the Registration Application ID for tracking.

### Offline Method (Via Common Service Centre / Agriculture Office)
1. Approach the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or local Village Nodal Officer / Revenue Patwari.
2. Submit self-attested photocopies of Aadhaar, Land Record (7/12 or RoR), and Bank Passbook.
3. CSC operator completes digital enrollment and biometric e-KYC.
