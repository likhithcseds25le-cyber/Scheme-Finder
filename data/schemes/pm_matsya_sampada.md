# Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)

**Ministry / Department:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India  
**Scheme Category:** Fisheries, Aquaculture, Rural Livelihood, Capital Subsidy  
**Official Website:** https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in  
**Scheme ID:** GOV-IN-FISH-010  
**Last Updated:** 2024-2025  

---

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objective
Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) is a landmark initiative to bring about the 'Blue Revolution' through sustainable and responsible development of fisheries sector in India. It supports fishers, fish farmers, fish workers, and fisheries cooperatives with high capital subsidy to establish aquaculture ponds, biofloc units, recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS), hatcheries, cold chains, and fishing gear.

## 2. Benefit Amount and Subsidy Structure
- **Subsidy for General Category:** **40% of unit project cost** as government financial assistance.
- **Subsidy for SC/ST and Women Beneficiaries:** **60% of unit project cost** as government financial assistance.
- **Project Examples & Indicative Assistance:**
  - Establishment of New Freshwater Finfish Hatcheries: Unit cost up to Rs. 25 Lakhs (Subsidy Rs. 10 Lakhs to 15 Lakhs).
  - Construction of New Ponds for Brackishwater/Freshwater Aquaculture: Unit cost Rs. 7 Lakhs/hectare (Subsidy 40% - 60%).
  - Biofloc Culture Units & RAS (Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems): Up to Rs. 25 - 50 Lakhs.
  - Insulated Ice Boxes & Motorized Three-Wheelers: Unit cost Rs. 3 Lakhs (Subsidy 40% - 60%).
  - Livelihood & Nutritional Support during fishing ban/lean period: Rs. 4,500 direct financial support.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
1. **Target Beneficiaries:**
   - Individual Fishers, Fish Farmers, Fish Workers, and Fish Vendors.
   - Fisheries Development Corporations, SHGs (Self Help Groups), and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs).
   - Fisheries Cooperatives and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
   - SC, ST, and Women entrepreneurs in fisheries sector.
2. **Land/Water Body Requirements:**
   - Ownership or registered lease of land/pond/water body for a minimum period of 7 to 10 years.
3. **Residency & Age:** Indian resident aged 18 years and above.

## 4. Mandatory Documents Required
1. **Identity & Address Proof:** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID.
2. **Land / Water Body Record:** Land ownership deed or registered lease agreement of water body.
3. **Caste Certificate:** SC or ST certificate (if claiming 60% subsidy).
4. **Detailed Project Report (DPR):** Technical project report with cost estimation, civil layout, and projected yield.
5. **Bank Account Details:** Bank passbook copy with IFSC and branch code.
6. **Fisher / Farmer Identity Card:** If registered with State Fisheries Department.

## 5. Step-by-Step Application Procedure
1. **Online Application via PMMSY Portal:**
   - Visit https://pmmsy.dof.gov.in.
   - Click on 'Citizen Registration / Apply Online'.
2. **Registration and Scheme Component Selection:**
   - Register using Aadhaar and mobile number.
   - Browse beneficiary-oriented activities and select the desired component (e.g., New Pond Construction, Biofloc unit, Cold Chain vehicle).
3. **Upload DPR and Land Records:**
   - Complete personal profile, bank information, and upload DPR along with land lease and cost estimates.
4. **District Fisheries Office Appraisal:**
   - The application is reviewed by the District Level Committee (DLC) chaired by District Collector / District Fisheries Officer (DFO).
   - Physical site verification is conducted by Fisheries Extension Officer.
5. **Sanction and Phased Subsidy Release:**
   - State Level Approval Committee (SLAC) approves the project.
   - Subsidy is released directly into the beneficiary's bank account in milestone-linked tranches via DBT.
